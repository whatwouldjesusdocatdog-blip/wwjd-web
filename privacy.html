Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

Cana is a Catholic Bible reading, study, and small-group app. This policy explains what we collect, what we don't, and who else is involved.

It's written to be specific rather than exhaustive. If anything here is unclear, email us — the address is at the bottom.

  • Your bookmarks, devotionals, devotion plans, private notes, and Ask conversations are stored in your own iCloud account. We cannot read them.
  • Your groups — names, prayer requests, shared notes — are stored on our servers, because other members need to see them.
  • Cana uses two AI services. Messages you send in Ask go to Anthropic, which doesn't use them to train AI models. Devotionals, devotion plans and group studies are written by Magisterium AI, which may keep what it receives under its own policy. We never send either service your name, email, or account.
  • We never see your payment details. Apple handles all purchases.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't run advertising.

What we store, and where

In your iCloud — not visible to us

Bookmarks, devotional sessions, devotion plans, saved Ask conversations, private session notes, and challenge commitments sync through Apple CloudKit using your own iCloud account — the same mechanism Apple's own apps use.

We have no access to this data. It isn't on our servers, and we cannot retrieve it, including if you ask us to.

On our servers — Google Firebase

We use Firebase (Google Cloud) for accounts and group features:

WhatWhy
An account identifier, and your email if you signed in with AppleTo identify you across devices
Display name and profile photoShown to other members of your groups
Groups, member lists, invite codesGroup membership
Session content: passages, icebreakers, prayer requests, shared notes, challengesVisible to everyone in that group
Subscription status and usage countsTo provide Cana Plus and apply free-tier limits

If you use Sign in with Apple and choose Hide My Email, we only ever receive Apple's relay address, never your real one.

Group content is not private. Anything you post in a group session — a prayer request, a shared note — is visible to every member of that group. Notes you mark private stay on your device and in your iCloud, and are never uploaded.

AI features

Cana uses two AI services to write its responses. Requests go through our own server, so your device never contacts either service directly, and we never send your name, email address, or account identifier — only the text needed for the response.

Anthropic (Claude)

Used for Ask, and for verse explanations that aren't already bundled with the app. What's sent: your Ask messages and the conversation so far, or the verse being explained.

  • Anthropic does not use this content to train AI models.

Magisterium AI (Longbeard)

Used for devotionals, devotion plans, and group study guides, which it writes from the Church's documents and the Fathers. What's sent:

  • for a devotional: the Ask conversation it's made from, so it can speak to what you're going through;
  • for a devotion plan: the topic you choose, and the conversation too if you made the plan from one;
  • for a group study: the Bible passages chosen for the session.

Magisterium AI processes requests with its own AI providers (including Microsoft Azure OpenAI, Google Gemini and Groq), which its policy says do not train on them and may keep them for up to 30 days. Magisterium AI may store what it receives and use it to improve its service, as described in its privacy policy. If you'd rather your conversations weren't shared this way, don't create devotionals or plans from them — reading, Ask, and the rest of Cana work without it.

If Magisterium AI is unavailable, the same request is sent to Anthropic instead.

What we keep

We do not store your Ask conversations or AI responses on our servers. Your conversations, devotionals and plans live in your iCloud; our server keeps only a count of how many messages you've sent, for free-tier limits. Group study guides are stored with the group, so its members can see them.

Cana is not a substitute for pastoral care, counselling, or medical or mental health treatment. If you tell it something suggesting you may be at risk of harm, it will surface crisis resources. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

Analytics

We use PostHog to understand how the app is used. We record events like app opens, sign-ins, and which features are used — not the content of what you read, write, or ask.

Payments

Subscriptions are purchased through Apple. We never see your card details. Apple tells our server whether a subscription is active, and when it renews, expires, or is refunded, so we know what to unlock. That's the extent of it.

Children

Cana isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your choices

  • Delete your account. In the app, open the profile menu and tap Delete Account. It permanently removes your profile and your contributions; groups you lead stay for their other members, and a group's record of having met may remain. It doesn't cancel a subscription — only Apple can.
  • AI features. Cana asks your permission before it first sends anything you write to its AI services, and you can turn this off any time under AI & Privacy in the profile menu. Reading, the daily readings and saints, and the bundled verse explanations work either way.
  • Delete your iCloud data. Because it's in your own iCloud, you control it directly — delete the app's data in your device's iCloud settings, or delete the app.
  • Analytics. Turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track, or contact us to opt out.
  • Access a copy. Email us and we'll provide what's stored on our servers.
